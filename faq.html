01 — The Site

About Shakuntalites

Shakuntalites is the official website of शाकुन्तलम् विद्यालय, Banasthali Vidyapith. It shares information about the school, its students and teachers, and preserves photographs and stories from our events — so that these years are never forgotten.

This site was designed and developed by Lukveer Singh and Rohit Yadav — two Shakuntalites who wanted to build something lasting for their batch. It is entirely student-made, non-commercial, and built with love.

No. Shakuntalites is an independent, student-run project. It is not affiliated with or officially endorsed by Banasthali Vidyapith. For the official school website, please visit banasthali.org.

We have built this site on GitHub Pages, which is free and reliable for long-term hosting. We intend to keep it online for as long as possible so that Shakuntalites can return to it for years to come. There are no plans to take it down.
