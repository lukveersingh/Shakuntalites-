Our School

A Place to Grow

Shakuntalam Vidyalaya, part of Banasthali Vidyapith, is built on the philosophy of Panchmukhi Shiksha — a five-fold education of the body, mind, intellect, heart, and soul. From Class 6 to Class 12, students are guided by dedicated teachers across four houses: Savitri, Gargi, Maitreyi, and Lopamudra.

This website is your window into our school — our events, our people, and the moments that make up everyday life here. Browse our photo galleries, meet our teachers, and get in touch with us any time.
